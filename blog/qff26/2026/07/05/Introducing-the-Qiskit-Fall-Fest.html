Introducing the Qiskit Fall Fest

← Back to blog

Published 5 Jul 2026 · Alberto Miserendino

“

Qiskit Fall Fest is a global lineup of community-led quantum computing events, including:

  • Hands-on workshops
  • Coding challenges and hackathons
  • Research talks and panels
  • Social and networking events

IBM Quantum partners with selected student leaders and volunteers each year to help plan and execute these events worldwide.

”
IBM Quantum

The Qiskit Fall Fest is a celebration of quantum technology, research, and collaboration, bringing together quantum enthusiasts, researchers, students, and professionals from around the world. Whether you’re a quantum expert or just starting your journey into the quantum realm, this event is designed for you.

Event highlights

  1. Cutting-edge quantum talks. Join renowned experts in quantum computing for keynote speeches, panel discussions, and technical presentations. Explore the latest advancements in quantum hardware, software, and applications.

  2. Hands-on workshops. Explore quantum programming in practical sessions led by Qiskit experts and learn how to build quantum circuits and algorithms with Qiskit.

  3. Hackathon challenge. Form a team, apply your quantum skills to practical problems and develop a working solution.

  4. Networking opportunities. Meet quantum enthusiasts, researchers and professionals to exchange ideas and find potential collaborators.

  5. Cultural excursions. Discover Palermo, its culture, and its historical landmarks.

Why attend?

  • Gain valuable insights into the future of quantum computing.
  • Learn from the best in the quantum industry.
  • Connect with like-minded individuals and expand your quantum network.
  • Participate in the quantum hackathon and sharpen your skills.
