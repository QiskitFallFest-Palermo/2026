FAQ

The event is open to everyone. You can register here.

No prior knowledge of quantum computing is required, although a basic understanding of linear algebra will make the topics easier to follow. If you are unfamiliar with the principles of quantum mechanics, don’t worry: we will introduce them briefly.

The event will also be streamed and recorded. Further information about remote participation will be provided after registration. We strongly encourage attending in person for a more engaging experience and better networking opportunities. Registered attendees will be notified by email when recordings become available.

You can follow us on our socials.

You have several transportation options:

  • 🚌 Airport buses run every 30 minutes from 04:00 to 22:30 (more information is available here).
  • 🚄 Regional trains run every 30 minutes from the airport to Palermo Centrale between 05:15 and 00:25.
  • 🚗 By car, take the A29 motorway towards Palermo.
  • 🚕 Taxis are available outside International Arrivals.

To reach us directly, take the train to 🚏 Palermo Centrale. The event venue is approximately 300 metres from the station.

Guests are welcome, but please make sure that they register because places at the venue are limited.
