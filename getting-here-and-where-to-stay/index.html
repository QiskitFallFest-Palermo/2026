Getting here and where to stay

Joining Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 @ Palermo in person? The venue is at Via Archirafi 36, Palermo, a short walk from Palermo Centrale railway station. This guide will help you plan your journey and choose a convenient place to stay.

Finding the venue

Use the full street address when planning your route: Via Archirafi 36, Palermo. From Palermo Centrale, follow walking directions to the entrance at number 36.

Step-free access to the venue is available for anyone who needs it.

From Palermo Airport

  • By train: search for a regional service from Palermo Aeroporto (Punta Raisi) to Palermo Centrale on Trenitalia, then walk to the venue.
  • By airport bus: Prestia e Comandè serves the airport and Palermo Centrale. Choose the central-station stop and check the timetable for your direction of travel.
  • By taxi: use the official taxi rank outside Arrivals and give the driver the address Via Archirafi 36, Palermo. Confirm the fare before departure.
  • By car: follow the A29 motorway towards Palermo, then directions to Via Archirafi 36. Read the parking advice below before setting off.

Check current departure times and fares with the operator before travelling, especially for early or late flights. RFI has announced timetable changes, cancellations and replacement buses on the airport line from 4 November 2026; check whether your chosen service is affected.

Travelling by train

Book your journey to Palermo Centrale, the most convenient railway station for the venue. Regional trains connect Palermo with other Sicilian destinations, while Intercity services provide connections from mainland Italy. Check journey times, changes and service notices for your travel date. From Centrale, continue on foot to Via Archirafi 36.

Travelling by pullman

For regional or long-distance coaches, look for a service arriving at Palermo Centrale / Via Fazello bus terminal, beside the railway station. For example, SAIS Autolinee lists services to Palermo and their arrival stops. Confirm the exact stop with your operator, as some services terminate elsewhere in the city.

Getting around Palermo by AMAT bus

AMAT operates Palermo's urban buses and trams. For connections from your accommodation or another part of the city, check the current routes and timetables and look for a service towards Stazione Centrale. From there, continue on foot to Via Archirafi 36. Check the direction and stop location before boarding, and allow extra time for traffic.

Buy your ticket from an authorised retailer or an AMAT sales point before travelling, and follow the validation instructions for your ticket. See AMAT's bus information for ticket-purchase details and the municipal bus and tram guide for further information.

Driving and parking

Parking at the venue is extremely limited. We recommend arriving on foot or by public transport where possible. If you drive, plan to use a public car park and allow time to walk to the entrance; do not rely on finding a space at the venue.

Palermo has a restricted traffic zone (ZTL) in the historic centre. Check the current boundaries, access times and permit requirements for your route and accommodation before driving. Ask your accommodation provider about parking and any access arrangements.

Accommodation recommendations

For convenient access to the event, we suggest starting your search in these areas and checking the walking route from each property's exact address:

  • Palermo Centrale: choose accommodation near the station if you want to walk to the venue and keep airport and rail connections close by.
  • Via Roma: consider accommodation along Via Roma, especially towards Palermo Centrale, for a central base with access to the station and the venue. Check the property's exact address and walking distance before booking, as Via Roma is a long street.
  • Kalsa: consider this historic neighbourhood if you would also like to explore the old town during your stay. Check the walking route from the property's exact address to Via Archirafi 36 before booking.

The City of Palermo accommodation directory lists hotels, B&Bs, hostels and other types of accommodation. Compare current availability, total prices, cancellation terms and recent reviews. Confirm late check-in if your flight arrives in the evening, and ask the property directly about accessibility or parking if needed.
